❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Q Do I need a Japanese credit card or guarantor?
No. A student ID (在学証明書) is all you need. We accept payments via convenience store or bank transfer. No Japanese credit card, no guarantor, no Japanese bank account required.
Q What if an appliance breaks down?
Contact us via LINE anytime (24h). We'll repair or replace it for free — usually within 1–2 days. Repair costs are fully covered in your monthly fee.
Q Can I use it outside Tokyo / Kanagawa?
Free delivery & pickup covers Tokyo 23 wards and Kanagawa Prefecture. Students in adjacent areas — please contact us and we'll check availability.
